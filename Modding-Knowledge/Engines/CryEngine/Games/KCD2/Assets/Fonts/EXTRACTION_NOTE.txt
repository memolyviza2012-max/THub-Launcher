No installable TTF/OTF is contained in the KCD2 Thai mod.

The supplied assets are Scaleform GFx resources, retained here as research/repacking assets:
- gfxfontlib.gfx: 67,261 B, magic CFX 08, zlib begins at offset 0x08 (78 DA)
- Modern/gfxfontlib_glyphs.gfx: 3,194,100 B, magic CFX 0F, zlib begins at 0x08 (78 9C)
- Ancient/gfxfontlib_glyphs.gfx: 5,409,415 B, magic CFX 0F, zlib begins at 0x08 (78 9C)

GFx is the game runtime resource; it does not expose a standalone TrueType/OpenType face to carve. Renaming these files to TTF would create invalid fonts and destroy the relationship between Scaleform font definitions and glyph data.
