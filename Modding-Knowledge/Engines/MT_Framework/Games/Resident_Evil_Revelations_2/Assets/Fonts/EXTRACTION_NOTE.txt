Resident Evil Revelations 2 — font extraction note (2026-09-13)

Source mod has 11 loose font assets: 7 TEX image atlases and 4 GFD metric/
glyph descriptor files. All are preserved in this directory. Each TEX was
converted on a copy with ARCtool 0.9.713 (-tex -texRE6) to a DDS in
../Textures/. DDS headers validate as 1024x512 or 1024x1024 DXT5/DXT1.

No installable TTF/OTF was found. The full mod, including all 4,519 inflated
ARC entries, was scanned for SFNT signatures and table directories; valid
SFNT candidates = 0. Bitmap pixels cannot recover original vector outlines,
OpenType shaping tables, designer metadata or licence. The README names
Sarabun and cites SIL OFL; the exact source font file/version is not present.

GFD magic: 47 46 44 00 (GFD\0), followed by 06 0C 01 00.
TEX magic: 54 45 58 00 (TEX\0), followed by 9D 20 00 20.
The GFD glyph record/UV/kerning layout has not been validated. Do not inject
coordinates at guessed byte offsets or treat a DDS as a drop-in replacement
for the game TEX. Preserve corresponding TEX and GFD as a set.
